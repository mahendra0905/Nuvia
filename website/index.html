How it works

Three moves, then it is just there.

No account creation, no api keys to paste. Nuvia signs in with the Telegram account you already have.

  1. Sign in with Telegram

    Enter your phone, the code Telegram sends you, and your two-step password if you set one. That is the whole login.

  2. Send a file up

    Pick a file and press upload. It lands in your Saved Messages, or in one private group you can create on your account.

  3. Find it whenever

    Your uploads show in a searchable list. Filter by name, then download any file back to your PC in a click.

Built to be honest

It reads nothing it does not need to.

Nuvia stores files and finds them again. It deliberately leaves the rest of your Telegram account alone, and it tells you the truth when something cannot be done.

  • Your index stays local

    The list of what you uploaded lives in a file on your PC and is never sent anywhere.

  • No chat snooping

    Nuvia never browses your conversations, exports contacts, or sends messages on your behalf.

  • Local rename is local

    Renaming a file changes only its label on your PC. The remote message and file are untouched.

  • Nothing happens without a click

    No upload, no group, no message ever starts on its own. You act, then Nuvia acts.

  • Uninstall keeps your data

    Removing Nuvia leaves your local files in place unless you explicitly opt in to delete them.

Questions worth asking.

Is this an official Telegram app?

No. Nuvia is an independent, unofficial application that uses the Telegram API. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Telegram.

Do I need to enter an api_id or api_hash?

No. Nuvia ships with what it needs to connect. You sign in with your normal Telegram phone number and login code, nothing else.

How large a file can I store?

Telegram's own limits apply: up to 2 GB per file on a free account, or 4 GB with Telegram Premium. Nuvia does not add a limit of its own.

Does deleting the app delete my files?

Uninstalling keeps your local data by default. Your files stay in your Telegram account either way. Nuvia never deletes remote files for you.

Will it show every file already in my Telegram?

No. Nuvia lists only the files it uploaded from this PC. It does not scan or mirror your Telegram history.
